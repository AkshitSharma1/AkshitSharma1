# Hi, I'm Akshit Sharma 👋

I'm an SDE at Oracle, working on agentic AI and generative AI systems. My research interests lie in AI Safety and Trustworthy AI, particularly understanding, evaluating, and improving the reliability of language models and AI agents

Recent work involves developing and evaluating LLM-powered applications, with a growing focus on robustness, safety, and reliability. My interests include AI evaluation, agent behaviour, oversight, and the trustworthy deployment of advanced AI systems

Earlier research focused on multimodal learning, misinformation analysis, and harmful-content detection. This shaped my interest in how AI systems behave in ambiguous, adversarial, and high-risk settings, and led to papers accepted at EMNLP (Findings and workshop), CVPR Workshops, WACV, and CVIP

I graduated from IIT Guwahati in 2025 with a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (minor in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence). I was also runner-up for the Dr. Shankar Dayal Sharma Gold Medal (overall excellence in academic performance, character, conduct, extracurricular activities, and social service)

Open to research collaborations, volunteering, and judging opportunities in these areas
