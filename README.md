# Hi, I'm Akshit Sharma 👋

I'm a Member of Technical Staff at Oracle, working on agentic AI and GenAI systems. I graduated from IIT Guwahati in 2025 with a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (minor in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence)

My research interests lie in AI safety and trustworthy AI. Recent work includes LLM red-teaming, safeguard evaluation, and studying the behaviour of coding agents. Earlier research focused on multimodal learning, misinformation analysis, and explainable hateful meme classification

## Selected work

1. **[ReplayLab](https://github.com/AkshitSharma1/ReplayLab)**: Replays coding-agent trajectories inside SWE-bench environments and records command outputs, test-file changes, and repository diffs to help inspect agent behaviour
2. **[Agentic Red-Teaming Framework](https://github.com/AkshitSharma1/Agentic-Red-Teaming-Framework)**: Automated single and multi-turn attacks against LLM safeguards, with structured reports and LLM-as-a-judge evaluation
3. **Multimodal research**: Papers accepted at EMNLP 2026 Findings, WiNLP at EMNLP 2026, CVPR Workshops (2024 and 2026), WACV 2026, and CVIP 2025. [View publications](https://www.akshit.cc/publications)
